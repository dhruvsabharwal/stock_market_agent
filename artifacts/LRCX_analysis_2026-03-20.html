1 · Executive Summary

Lam Research is a global leader in wafer fabrication equipment, specialising in plasma etch, deposition, and clean technologies critical to advanced semiconductor manufacturing. Nearly every advanced chip produced today involves Lam tooling at some stage of its fabrication, giving the company deep customer stickiness across leading logic, memory, and foundry fabs.

Moat: High switching costs embedded in process recipes, proprietary Equipment Intelligence® software, and an installed base of over 100,000 chambers generating recurring CSBG (Customer Support Business Group) revenue that reached a record $7.2B in 2025 — insulating the business from pure cyclicality.

Competitive position: Dominant in conductor etch alongside KLA (KLAC) and Applied Materials (AMAT). SAM share of WFE has expanded into the mid-thirties percent range vs. a multi-year goal of high-thirties. Advanced packaging (HBM, 3D stacking) is expected to grow >40% in 2026.
